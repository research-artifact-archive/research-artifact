# Retry synthesis anonymous paper

`main.pdf` is *Retry Synthesis with Bounded Calls and Protected Work*. It has 18 pages through the complete Conclusion and 20 pages including Data Availability and references. `SNAPSHOT.json` binds its exact PDF/source bytes and immutable evidence commit.

The manuscript gives a charged atomic-call normal form, a universal call/protected-work frontier without a writer-budget input, exact post-threshold feasibility conditions, all-budget slope construction, a compact mixed-price class, and a sharp independent-job ratio. Minimum-ready-work selection is optimal within its threshold family. Two certified protection refinements weakly improve worst work over threshold, but neither dominates the other; general work-optimality remains open. The [suffix certificate](../charged/SUFFIX_CERTIFICATE.md) and [research lineage](../charged/RESEARCH_LINEAGE.md) provide proofs, scope and attribution.

The source studies include Roslyn's text-update path, a 4,019-document source projection and controlled Java collateral blocking. Roslyn call/transformation counts transfer under the stated normal-execution assumptions; variable/lazy costs do not satisfy the work theorem. Timings mostly do not improve. The old reentry timeout and corrected no-op case, failed mean-time calibration, limited Deephaven import and all unfavorable results remain preserved. These results do not establish editor performance, natural-arrival latency or human benefit.
