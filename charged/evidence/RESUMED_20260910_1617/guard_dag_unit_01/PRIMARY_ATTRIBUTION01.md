# Closest primary source for Candidate01

M. R. Garey and D. S. Johnson, “Scheduling Tasks with Nonuniform Deadlines on Two Processors,” Journal of the ACM23(3),461–467,1976. DOI https://doi.org/10.1145/321958.321967. The relevant result is **Theorem2, pp.465–466**, on one processor despite the paper title.

The source already reduces CLIQUE using unit-time vertex and edge jobs, endpoint precedence, vertex deadline |V|+|E|, edge deadline k+choose(k,2), and at most |E|−choose(k,2) tardy jobs. Its prefix argument and four-part constructive order match the combinatorial core of Candidate01. This is inherited scheduling hardness, including the two-level incidence structure; Candidate01 must cite the source at the reduction, not imply a new generic scheduling construction.

The completion-specific obligation is to connect all-program resource caps to this selection: the total cap constrains cached-edge positions, the protected cap forces enough cached edges, and the positive construction bounds every first-mismatch path including fresh-prefix work. The all-call-fee translation and the common-guard frontier bound have separate proofs. The new application’s significance and novelty remain for evaluation; this source check does not certify either.
