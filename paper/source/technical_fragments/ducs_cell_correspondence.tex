DUCS's $T$ constrains one global stop/start pair, including overlap, gaps and their duties~\cite[Def.~4.1]{Nahabedian2020}. FG separates individual stops and starts/initializers.

\paragraph{Cell through native interfaces.}
DUCS permits staged reconfiguration inside $E'$~\cite[Sec.~4.2]{Nahabedian2020}. Technical Appendices~\ref{ta:ducs_cell_complete}--\ref{ta:gr1-cell} give chosen Cell encodings: DUCS adds eventual handover beyond its safety-$T$ implementation~\cite[Sec.~5.3]{Nahabedian2020}; GR(1) may restrict endpoint edges when synthesizing $C_2$. These controllable, deterministic witnesses establish neither general translation nor cost advantage (S5).
