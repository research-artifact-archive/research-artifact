\paragraph{Independent requirement boundaries.}
DUCS's transition requirement $T$ constrains one global stop/start pair, including overlap, gaps and their duties~\cite[Def.~4.1]{Nahabedian2020}. FG-DUCS gives each requirement its own stop, start and initial obligation.

\paragraph{Cell through native interfaces.}
DUCS permits staged reconfiguration inside its supplied new environment $E'$~\cite[Sec.~4.2]{Nahabedian2020}. Technical Appendices~\ref{ta:ducs_cell_complete}--\ref{ta:gr1-cell} give chosen Cell encodings: our DUCS Cell encoding adds eventual handover beyond the safety-$T$ implementation~\cite[Sec.~5.3]{Nahabedian2020}; GR(1) may restrict endpoint edges when synthesizing its new controller. In these encodings, the modeler writes the stages and boundaries into the supplied environment or predicates. FG-DUCS generates them from local inputs. These controllable, deterministic witnesses establish neither a general translation nor a cost advantage (S5).
