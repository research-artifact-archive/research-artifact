# Post-outcome analysis of the saved arrival timelines

This is a new descriptive analysis of already observed, fixed source executions, prompted by the limited PDF check129. It is neither a new native campaign nor a preregistration of the original outcomes. Keep all four prior campaigns and every raw unit: initial arrivals864, position-balanced arrivals1728, sequential-rebase arrivals3072, and batch-rebase arrivals1728. Preserve warmups separately from measurements; no status or unit is excluded. The source checker and raw hashes remain prerequisites, not new independent semantic evidence.

First reconstruct B_fg, the number of background publications during the recorded foreground interval, from both publication events and writer request timestamps. Separate it from all64 requested/completed writes. B_fg is a retrospective count for a particular execution; it is not supplied to the controller and can itself depend on the chosen policy's completion time. It is not the number of writes specifically overlapping read/comparison intervals. Recheck Q=32+cheap failures, Q<=32+r for capped arms, and the source count bound inside<=min(max(B_fg-r,0),32) for three/rebase/batch. Report every violation, including mismatched timestamps.

For each campaign, phase, mode, r, and arrival interval, report all unit counts and B_fg's minimum/median/maximum, histogram and proportions below r, between r and r+32, and at/above r+32. Also retain per-unit and aggregate protected-transform counts, calls, foreground duration, and background request p95 from intended arrival to return. Full-transform counts are not protected work in time units. The per-execution upper bound is not an observed worst-case optimum, and different policies' retrospective B_fg counts do not constitute a same-B controlled comparison.

Only the batch campaign has nonblocking foreground phase timestamps. Reconstruct its lock-enter-to-terminal-hook spans, excluding acquisition and release. Verify ordering, disjointness, correspondence to call counters, containment within the foreground interval, and absence of a background publication strictly inside these spans. Compute their total observed duration and their overlap with each background invocation-to-return interval. This overlap cannot identify time spent waiting on the semaphore: the latter interval also contains background computation and notification work. No causal regression, SLA, general monotonicity or calibrated per-call cost is inferred.

As descriptive associations, compute within-cell Spearman ranks for observed protected duration versus the background p95 and versus foreground duration in measurement batches, with average ranks for ties and undefined constant series explicitly retained. Also report the eight pooled batch/rebase timing directions from the original paired design. No p-values, significance selection or pooled causal interpretation. Five corruption controls target B_fg, a background publication timestamp, an excessive protected counter, a missing lock entry, and an out-of-range phase. All results and exceptions are saved once.
