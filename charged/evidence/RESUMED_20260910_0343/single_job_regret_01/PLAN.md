The published137 whole-kernel threshold-policy bound is at most rκ relative to the B-informed comparator and is sharp for that policy. It does not assert minimax regret over every unaware program. This unit asks the latter question for exactly one positive-work job, before any finite-check outcomes.

The B-informed comparator is the full program class of Theorem1, with its cap on environments of at most B writes. Conjectured informed value is κ+min(Bκ,w) for 0≤B≤r, and κ+w for B>r. Conjectured minimax additive regret is min(max(w−κ,0),rκ). The candidate policies are always fresh preparation/cached completion, or r cheap failures followed by freshly prepared cached completion. For r≥1 and κ>0, an intermediate switching threshold should not improve this regret. A first fresh completion must be considered, including its zero-write penalty.
