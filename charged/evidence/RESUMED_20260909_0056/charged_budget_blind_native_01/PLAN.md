# One policy without a writer-budget input

SCIENTIFIC exploratory successor, fixed before native execution. Parent `charged_resource_frontier_native_01` and all its results stay unchanged. Test the stronger claim that a single policy using a call allowance r, but no writer bound B and no work weights, attains the three-mode protected-work frontier for every finite B simultaneously. Policy: choose the least ready mandatory job; use cheap optimistic validation until r cheap failures have occurred globally; thereafter use cached completion. The two-mode comparison switches instead to fresh protected completion. Successful cheap calls do not consume the allowance. Both policies make at most n+r charged calls under every finite writer schedule. The old budget-aware two-mode value is zero at B=r; the proposed universal-Q two-mode value is total work already at B=r, because the policy cannot rule out one further invalidation. This is a distinct policy class and the boundary difference must be preserved.
