\paragraph{Concrete Cell constructions.}
Our DUCS witness supplies eight mixed stages, three endpoint states, empty-station guards, inspection/lifetime fluents and a target gate; DUCS generates its protocol. The GR(1) witness supplies version/lifetime variables, $p'=[h=B]$ at start, release at stop and a target test; its algorithm generates switching bookkeeping. Both admit the Cell paths; the appendices distinguish their endpoint guarantees. Technical Appendices~\ref{ta:ducs_cell_complete} and~\ref{ta:gr1-cell} give the full analytical constructions, including the DUCS eventual-handover condition beyond native safety-$T$~\cite[Sec.~5.3]{Nahabedian2020}. These are neither solver runs nor general translations or encoding-size lower bounds.
