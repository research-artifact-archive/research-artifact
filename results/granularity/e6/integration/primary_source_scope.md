# E6 primary-source scope

Verified on 2026-09-29. `primary_sources.bib` contains ten entries for the sources already used by E6. This file separates the source's support from the finite construction's assumptions; it adds no manuscript claims. Undated documentation has no `year` field. A version number, copyright year, update timestamp, or URL directory was not treated as a publication year. The access date is 2026-09-29 throughout.

| BibTeX key | Verified title, responsible organization, and date | Source support | Construction assumptions and limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| `e6_kubernetes_deployments` | **Deployments**, Kubernetes documentation; publication year not established. [Official page](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/workloads/controllers/deployment/). | The Rolling Update Deployment / Max Unavailable / Max Surge sections describe controlled replacement, an unavailable-replica budget, and extra-replica limits. Readiness and availability participate in rollout progression. | Rolling's fixed population, one-step uncontrollable readiness, no permanent startup wait, sequential transfer groups, exact threshold, and strong-completion guarantee are model choices. The finite LTS is not a Kubernetes implementation. |

The author's publication page establishes article 13 and October 2014. The manuscript front page has a placeholder DOI and volume fields; those placeholders were not copied into the bibliography. The title/subtitle and DOI in the BibTeX entry use the verified registry metadata, while the technical claims use the manuscript text. NIST's author record and publisher-deposited DOI metadata also agree on Kuhn; the latter supplies the expanded given name D. Richard.

These are operational or technical anchors for constructed finite witnesses. They do not establish the experimental judgments, ranks, domain totality, success probabilities, scheduler fairness, or effectiveness in deployed systems. Those claims, when made, must come from the preserved inputs, semantic definitions, raw results, and certificate checks.
