\textbf{Events and state notation.} $m_A/m_B/j=$ move to $A$/move to $B$/inspect; $o/n=$ old/new, $h/e=$ holding/empty, $k/c/d/E/-=$ ok/clear/pending/error/inactive.
Each policy row gives both versions, holder (also the $r_{one}$ state), monitor states and rank.
Its pending set consists of each old component's transfer, start while the new monitor is inactive, and stop while the old monitor is active.
$\SigmaN=\{m_A,m_B,j\}$, $\SigmaU=\{\rho_A,\rho_B,\mathsf{start},\mathsf{stop}\}$, $\Sigmauc=\emptyset$ and $\mathsf{start}=\ustart{r_{inspect}}\prec\mathsf{stop}=\ustop{r_{old}}$.

\textbf{Transfers and monitoring.} $g_i=\{(e_i^o,e_i^n)\}$; bulk $g_A\times g_B$ requires unreachable $(A_e^o,B_e^o)$.
$R^{old/new/upd}=\{r_{old}\}/\{r_{inspect}\}/\{r_{one}\}$.
$\operatorname{dom}\iota_{one}$ is the two old one-product states; $\iota_{one}$ is the holder, with move-preserving observation (exact RS).
$\operatorname{dom}\iota_{inspect}$ is all eight tagged one-product states; $\iota_{inspect}=d$ at $B_h$, otherwise $c$.
Section~\ref{sec:monitor-meaning} specifies its activation-history interpretation and RS.

\textbf{Endpoints and policy.} The reachable old states are $x_A=(A_h^o,B_e^o,c_o,k)$ and $x_B=(A_e^o,B_h^o,c_o,k)$; the reachable new states are $z_A=(A_h^n,B_e^n,c,c)$, $y_B=(A_e^n,B_h^n,d,d)$ and $z_B=(A_e^n,B_h^n,c,c)$.
The initial endpoint states are $x_A,z_A$ and $Z_{load}=\{z_A,z_B\}$.
The displayed paths define one policy from $Q_0=\{\Init(x_A),\Init(x_B)\}$, empty elsewhere, with 13 states and 11 edges. Their last states map to $z_B,z_A$, respectively.

Unlisted plant/controller transitions are absent; unlisted non-error monitor transitions self-loop; $E$ absorbs every label. Comma-separated labels are separate edges. The four $\Post$ rules determine every update successor; S1 tabulates the unsafe-inclusive closure.
