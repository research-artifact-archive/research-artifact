\paragraph{Checking the returned evidence.}
WIN checking requires exactly $D_\sigma=\Reach_\sigma(Q_0)$ as the rank domain, safety, enabled selected controls, nonempty all-outcome closure and strict decrease at non-goals. Goals require rank zero, quiescence and a compatible $\kappa$, defined exactly on $D_\sigma\cap\Goal$; omitted policy entries are empty. LOSS checking requires an entry-containing, goal-free $L$ with the UC/all-control counter-outcomes used in the completeness proof. These certify search relative to the successor and endpoint predicates~\cite{HofmannEtAl2016MuCertificates}; Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} states their implementation trust boundary.
