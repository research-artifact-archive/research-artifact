\paragraph{GSM after activation.}
\label{sec:gsm-entry-derivation}
GSM changes $\Box\neg decode.3$ to $\Box\neg decode.2$. A source-derived all-entry policy starts the new requirement with observer/monitor zero, then permits $decode.3$ and output before transfer while rejecting $decode.2$. It reaches a matching endpoint within twelve events and satisfies R1's gap restriction. This proves ACT over ordinary behavior; PC2's active suffix contains only starts. The complete path, rank proof and separate 45-pair entry-initialization check are in Technical Appendix~\ref{ta:activation_scope}; no new solver result is claimed.
