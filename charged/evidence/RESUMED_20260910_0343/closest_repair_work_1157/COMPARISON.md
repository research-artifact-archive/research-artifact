Mokhov, Mitchell and Peyton Jones, **Build Systems à la Carte**, PACMPL2(ICFP), Article79,2018: Definitions2.1 and3.1 specify build minimality and correctness; Sections4–5 separate scheduling from rebuilding, using persistent metadata and dependency/value information. Section6.6 explicitly discusses bounded iteration to avoid nontermination. Reuse, task restarting and finite iteration are therefore inherited concepts. Their stated guarantees concern a consistent built store and avoiding unnecessary task execution. Our repair theorem instead selects accept/repair versus fresh retry after a dirty-union observation, subject to a universal call cap and hidden external-write budget. The inspected definitions and algorithms do not give our adversarial acceptance value or all-budget existence criterion. Primary: https://simon.peytonjones.org/assets/pdfs/build-systems-original.pdf .

Ley-Wild, Acar and Fluet, **A Cost Semantics for Self-Adjusting Computation**, POPL2009,186–199: Sections5.2–5.3, Theorems4–5, relate mutable-store change propagation to consistent evaluation and trace distance; Theorem5 equates the corresponding dynamic cost and distance and reports a Twelf proof. The source/target translation then supports cost reasoning from source traces. Thus formal repair-cost analysis and semantic preservation are prior results. Their distance compares reused/discarded/new computation between executions. Our controller observes a coarser dirty union and optimizes when to accept under a call cap and unknown write budget. This is a different information/decision contract; the inspected theorem is not that stopping theorem. Primary: https://ttic.uchicago.edu/~pl/sa-sml/popl09.pdf .
