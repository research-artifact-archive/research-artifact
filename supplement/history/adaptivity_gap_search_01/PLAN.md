Existing fixed-native 2/192 strict roots (max2.0833%) and old 128-case all-budget order study (max10/347) are already observed. This study does not replace or enlarge either population. It asks whether the small gaps are intrinsic to the model or admit stronger mathematical examples. A strong example alone establishes no application prevalence or runtime benefit.

Use the unchanged exact dependency_curves_01 curves as the exploratory oracle. For every evaluated DAG compute the adaptive all-budget curve and the lower envelope of all fixed topological-order curves. Each order can adapt protection choices to observed failures; its initial order may be chosen optimally for the initial budget. Set successful cost a=c. Score is the maximum over all integer budgets of (F-V)/(sum(c)+F), computed exactly at the union of curve boundaries. Retain every input, curve, candidate order count, status and timing. Best examples require a separately implemented scalar Bellman/order check and explicit argument before paper adoption.
