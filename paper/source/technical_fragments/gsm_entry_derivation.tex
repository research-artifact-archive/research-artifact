\paragraph{A communication protocol after activation.}
\label{sec:gsm-entry-derivation}
For the GSM-oriented communication protocol~\cite[Sec.~6.4]{Nahabedian2020}, a source-derived policy covers every entry for the change from $\Box\neg decode.3$ to $\Box\neg decode.2$. It activates the new requirement with both observer and monitor zero, permits $decode.3$ and output before transfer, and rejects $decode.2$. It satisfies R1's gap restriction and reaches a matching endpoint within twelve events, establishing the invariant from activation through ordinary behavior. Technical Appendix~\ref{ta:activation_scope}~\cite{FGDUCSAnonymousArtifact} gives the path, rank proof and separate 45-pair entry-initialization check.
