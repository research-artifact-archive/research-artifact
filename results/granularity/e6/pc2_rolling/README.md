# PC2-Rolling attempt

Preregistered in DAILY before generation/execution. Attempt window: 2026-09-29 16:29–18:29 JST, including the initial read-only design. Original plant source is read-only; all versions and failures stay here.

Proposed construction preserves the original ProductionCell operational transitions and adds a calibration-setting update protocol with a preparation state per existing physical stage. Calibration completion is uncontrollable; explicit recalibration requests are controllable. The interval and new endpoint contract require at least one arm ready.

Primary inspiration: Universal Robots, Resetting a calibration, which documents reboot after changing calibration settings: https://www.universal-robots.com/manuals/EN/HTML/SW5_19/Content/prod-rck/rck-resetting-the-calibration.htm . This is not a reproduction of a UR product or a claim that every software update requires physical calibration.

Final fixed-series status: Lazy/fine WIN; Lazy/merged transfers TO; Direct-Full/fine TO. The preregistered expectation was fine WIN / merged transfers LOSS. The two incomplete cells remain TO, and the generated input was not changed in response to these outcomes.

## Implemented version and validation

`v1/` preserves four design-preflight parse failures. The generator initially stopped at the `safety` block's inner closing brace and omitted the `controllable` field and outer brace. `v2/` fixes that extraction only. It preserves all 35,136 original source bytes as its prefix. `v1/gen_pc2.py` preserves the failed generator. A first diagnostic-wrapper compilation used an incorrect type name; `build/compile-v1.log` preserves that failure and `compile-v2.log` records the successful JDK 17 compilation against the unchanged E1 JAR.

Each arm's seven new operational states retains exactly its original outgoing ordinary transitions, adds a controllable `beginCalibration[i]` edge into a corresponding calibration state, and has one uncontrollable `calibrated[i]` return from that calibration state. The original seven transfer pairs now target these corresponding calibration states. The original old/new process declarations and 12 safety requirements per version remain intact. The new goal additionally forbids both arms calibrating; the interval requires at least one ready arm and observes both transfer and explicit recalibration requests.

`Calibrating` in the endpoint contract observes ordinary `beginCalibration`/`calibrated` history. `CalReady` in the interval also observes transfer actions. The fixed adapter carries ordinary fluent history across transfer; it does not advance ordinary observers on update actions. Thus these two monitors must not be identified during the update. Endpoint compatibility and activation are checked by the fixed E1 frontend.

`v2/validation/source_projection.json` records byte preservation and source-level erasure of only calibration additions. `v2/validation/endpoint_graphs.json` independently checks exported graph isomorphism and replays old/new endpoint traces against their original safety guards. The old endpoint has 81 states and 216 edges; the new endpoint has 243 states and 756 edges. Both are nonblocking and do not disable enabled uncontrollable plant actions. The new endpoint always has at least one operational arm. The validator's initial parser did not accept a final alphabet extension; that local parser correction and its failure are retained in `validator_parser_failure.json`, without changing the generated input or controller.

## Fixed measurement

`v2/build/frozen_manifest.json` fixes the generated input, generator, runner, checker, diagnostic Java source and class, and E1 JAR SHA. The one-shot schedule is Lazy/none, Lazy/transfers, Direct-Full/none, each with 32g heap and a 1,200 s whole-JVM limit. No new cell starts at or after 18:08 JST, preserving the 18:29 two-hour attempt limit. A global E6 file lock prevents concurrent solver JVMs. The measured series is under `v2/raw/`; design endpoint preflight logs are separate under `v2/preflight/`.

`Pc2DiagnosticRunner.java` is a thin same-package CLI bridge compiled to a class directory; no JAR is created. It calls the existing E1 CLI entry point and reads the existing public retained `Outcome` after synthesis. Its post-run diagnostics count the checked certificate's safe/unsafe and initial states and retain three local root-bucket examples. LOSS region size is the checked discovered-state certificate region, not the full game's complete losing region. WIN rank is the maximum over the returned strategy-reachable certificate, not a proven minimum completion length. Diagnostic time is separate, and still included in the whole-JVM timeout. A timeout retains all partial logs without fabricated state/rank/certificate values.

Calibration completion is a finite one-step uncontrollable abstraction. The model contains no forever-waiting calibration self-loop and assumes no scheduler fairness; it does not establish a completion guarantee for a physical robot. Availability refers to being outside the added calibration state, not to instantaneous production throughput or successful machining.

`validate_merge_obstruction.py` and `v2/validation/merge_obstruction.json` give a separate source-level obstruction: before the unique merged transfer both active versions are old and cannot execute either calibration action; the interval starts with both readiness bits true; the mandatory joint transfer resets both bits and enters two calibration states. Both orders of the merged tester observations reach error. This is a structural argument, not an E1 losing certificate. Any measured TO remains TO.

A separately frozen `validation_export/` adapter is reserved for validation-only replay after all three measurement cells terminate. It preserves complete certificate graphs and the linked graph for completed cells, rechecks the certificate and Link with E1, and compares the result against the original measurement. It uses a separate 180 s cap and never retries a timeout/error cell. Replay time and counts are validation diagnostics only; they do not replace the measured rows.

## Final results and files

The fixed series and its validation export completed by 17:51:47 JST, inside the two-hour attempt window. The completed Lazy/fine cell discovered 6,639 states, made 134,128 successor queries, and materialized 15,184 transition outcomes. Its returned certificate has 381 states, all 126 initial states, 522 strategy edges, and maximum rank 37. Its linked controller has 893 states and 2,316 edges. Preparation took 55.000430 s, synthesis plus the internal checker took 9.660384 s, and the whole JVM took 77.344180 s on the Mac. These are reference times, not Xeon comparisons. Timeout wall times include the termination grace period after the 1,200 s synthesis-process limit.

The validation-only replay saved the full certificate and linked graph in `v2/validation/export/lazy_none/certificate.json`; native E1 certificate and Link rechecks passed, and the result, state/query/outcome counts, rank, and certificate size exactly matched the measured run. `v2/validation/final_artifact_audit.json` independently checks the saved graph's rank decrease, exact strategy-reachable domain, coverage of all initial states, safe/available physical states, graph references, and raw/CSV/SHA agreement. Every saved certificate state has at least one operational arm. This Python check does not independently reimplement FSP Post or Link semantics. Both TO cells were skipped by export and retain partial logs.

`v2/tables/results.csv` contains the three measured rows, and `v2/tables/s4_pc2_calibration.tex` is the handoff fragment. Its standalone wrapper is `v2/tables/pc2_table_check.tex`. Source/endpoint projection validation, failure logs, raw invocations/completions, and the separate structural merged-transfer obstruction are retained in the locations described above. The structural obstruction does not replace either TO with a measured LOSS.

CSV columns: `job_id,model,solver,merge,status` identify the fixed cell; `states_discovered,successor_queries,materialized_transition_outcomes` are completed-run native counters. `enabled_buckets` is blank because the fixed E1 CLI did not directly instrument that distinct count, as explained by `enabled_buckets_availability`. `worst_completion_rank` is the maximum rank of the returned WIN certificate; `losing_region_states` is the size of a returned discovered-state LOSS certificate, not the full game's losing set. `certificate_states,unsafe_region_states,initial_states_in_certificate,initial_states` describe the checked certificate and initial coverage. `preparation_seconds,solve_and_internal_check_seconds,diagnostic_seconds,whole_jvm_seconds` keep the timing scopes separate. `input_sha256,jar_sha256,exit_code,reason,raw_directory` preserve provenance, status explanation, and the original logs. State/rank/certificate fields are blank for TO; blanks never mean zero.
