\paragraph{Cell through native interfaces.}
DUCS permits staged reconfiguration inside its supplied new environment $E'$~\cite[Sec.~4.2]{Nahabedian2020}. TA~\ref{ta:ducs_cell_complete}--\ref{ta:gr1-cell} give chosen Cell encodings that hold the same eight version/holder configurations, two transfers and two requirement boundaries fixed (Table~\ref{tab:cell-input-responsibilities}): our DUCS encoding adds an eventual handover event to $T$, beyond the published safety-$T$ implementation~\cite[Sec.~5.3]{Nahabedian2020}, and with its explicit post-handover graph also preserves endpoint traces; GR(1) may restrict endpoint edges when synthesizing its new controller. The native constructions supply their stage/guard equations, whereas FG-DUCS generates the operational structure from local inputs while still requiring monitors and initializers. These controllable, deterministic witnesses establish neither a general translation nor a cost advantage (S5).
