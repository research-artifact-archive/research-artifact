# One permitted failure removes precedence from the one-write joint frontier

New author deduction after general-r recurrence and existing finite results; fixed before the targeted formula extraction below. This is an analytic claim, not a new blind review or an unseen preregistration. Preserve the old r0 Lawler result and all general-r raw cases.

Claim: for a supplied B=1 and any r>=1, at total-work cap W<=Omega+M, M>=0, the least worst protected work over the whole program interface is

    L_1(M) = sum_(i:wi>M) wi.

It holds on every DAG. Let heavy mean wi>M. Execute any topological order, fresh on heavy jobs and immediate prepare/cheap on light jobs. After the first cheap failure, immediate prepare/cached every unfinished job, including the failed one. Within the supplied one-write budget there can be no later mismatch. Before that failure heavy jobs may have protected work; the no-write path protects exactly every heavy job. Thus worst L equals the heavy sum. There is at most one failed call, so Q<=n+1<=n+r; the only duplicate preparation has light weight<=M, so worst W<=Omega+M. Using cached after the first failure also preserves Q<=n+1 outside the promised write budget, without asserting the W/L caps there. The policy branches only on the cheap success/failure flag; it needs no cached comparison Boolean, even with fully erased constant outputs.

For a direct lower bound, follow any admissible program's no-write execution from the charged common start. If job i completes through a matching comparison, one fresh own-gate write gives an execution that paid a preparation for the old identity and still must pay another distinct kernel to complete i; failure at a cheap call postpones completion but cannot eliminate this extra wi. The environment never restores the old identity, the foreground cannot restore an unfinished input, and records cannot be retagged. Hence W<=Omega+M prevents any heavy job from completing through such a matching comparison on the no-write path. Every heavy job instead completes fresh or through a zero-write-stale cached operation, already protecting wi. Summing on that one no-write path gives L>=sum_heavy wi. Earlier stale-cheap failures and wasted preparations only strengthen the lower bound. This argument does not depend on the new general-r normal form.

The complete Pareto frontier is the nondominated threshold set (Omega+M,L_1(M)) for M=0 and the distinct job weights. It is computable by sorting in O(n log n)+topological ordering; as for the r0 case, a slack W coordinate is discarded if dominated. Further retry slack beyond one cannot improve this B1 frontier. At M>=wmax the value is zero and W=Omega+wmax. At M=0 it is Omega and W=Omega. The strict r0-vs-r1 gap at a fixed total-work cap is ell_Lawler(M)-sum_heavy wi>=0. It can depend on precedence only in the r0 term.
