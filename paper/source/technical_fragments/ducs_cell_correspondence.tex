\paragraph{Independent requirement boundaries.}
DUCS's transition requirement $T$ already expresses overlap, gaps and gap duties for one global stop/start pair~\cite[Sec.~2, Def.~4.1]{Nahabedian2020}. FG-DUCS separates each old stop and new start/initializer, as Policy(2)'s audit overlap and role exclusion require.

\paragraph{Cell through native interfaces.}
DUCS and GR(1) express Cell's paths with added structure. Technical Appendices~\ref{ta:ducs_cell_complete}--\ref{ta:gr1-cell} give the supplied stages, lifetime predicates and complete constructions; these are choices for Cell, not mandatory native input formats. The DUCS witness retains every endpoint edge after its added handover $h$ and requires eventual $h$, beyond the published safety-$T$ implementation~\cite[Sec.~5.3]{Nahabedian2020}; GR(1) may select fewer endpoint edges when synthesizing $C_2$. These controllable, deterministic examples imply neither general translation nor cost advantage. S5 details coverage and progress.
