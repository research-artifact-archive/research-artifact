Duties that depend on the past require \emph{residual inclusion}: every initialized-monitor continuation must satisfy the requirement after every history in its declared scope (Technical Appendix~\ref{ta:residual_formal}). Equality gives exactness; stricter inclusion can reject feasible updates. Unlike Cell's installed duty or an activation-scoped invariant, this interpretation depends on admitted histories. Their scope must contain the actual histories; an empty scope makes inclusion vacuous.

The saved checks use safe pre-activation histories consistent with the current observation for new requirements and a reference beginning at entry for interval requirements. A whole-history interpretation also includes prior violations and is considered separately (S3).
