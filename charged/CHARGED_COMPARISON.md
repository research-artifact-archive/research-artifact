# Comparison and protected-entry costs

This extension keeps three optimization contracts separate. [Proof03](evidence/RESUMED_20260910_1617/charged_comparison_01/PROOF03.md) gives (A) the supplied-one-write W/P_g scheduling reduction with body-only W, and (B) the universally admissible zero-write W_t/P_t subset frontier. [Fully charged proof05](evidence/RESUMED_20260910_1617/charged_comparison_01/FULLY_CHARGED_PROOF05.md) gives the supplied-one-write W_t/P_t frontier over all deterministic completion programs, represented by an order, a fresh/cached subset and a first-mismatch switch. Guard and comparison charges are paid in both coordinates in that latter class.

The all-program proof includes free inspections, old raw snapshots predating the counted writer interval, charged early preparations, retained records and value-dependent choices. No mandatory initialization tax is imposed. A cached mismatch recomputes and completes in the same call; the adversarial gate precedes its protected part. The proof takes separate worst-case resource suprema, which need not share a maximizing execution.

Joint-cap decision for the general supplied-one-write charged class is NP-complete for binary integer inputs, already for independent jobs. A polynomial-size order/mask certificate suffices; the exact frontier can have exponentially many points. A dummy guarded job embeds ordinary subset selection. Its large guard is a mathematical construction. The embedded subfamily is weakly hard with its inherited pseudopolynomial DP; the general charged class's strong/weak classification remains open. This does not establish hardness of the original free-comparison problem or a native-time calibration. [Cost boundary note05](evidence/RESUMED_20260910_1617/charged_comparison_01/COST_BOUNDARY_NOTE05.md) changes comparison costs while holding the embedded family fixed; it does not prove general homogeneous-cost tractability.
