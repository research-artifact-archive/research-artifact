\paragraph{GSM after activation.}
\label{sec:gsm-entry-derivation}
For GSM's change from $\Box\neg decode.3$ to $\Box\neg decode.2$, a source-derived policy covers every entry. It starts the new requirement with observer/monitor zero, permits $decode.3$ and output before transfer, and rejects $decode.2$. It satisfies R1's gap restriction and reaches a matching endpoint within twelve events, establishing the invariant from activation through ordinary behavior. Technical Appendix~\ref{ta:activation_scope}~\cite{FGDUCSAnonymousArtifact} gives the path, rank proof and separate 45-pair entry-initialization check.
