\subsection{A Constructive Correspondence for Cell}
\label{sec:ducs-cell-construction}
Native DUCS can express the two Cell paths~\cite[Defs.~4.1--4.3 and 5.1]{Nahabedian2020}. Its new environment contains eight mixed version/holder stages plus three new-endpoint states. Ordinary moves change the holder, empty-station transfers change one version, and a guarded handover connects all-new stages to clear-inspection targets. Holder refinement, history fluents and transition requirements enforce lifetimes. Technical Appendix~\ref{ta:ducs_cell_complete} proves path and continuation preservation; Table~\ref{tab:contract-responsibility} contrasts these explicit inputs with FG-DUCS's generated structure. The 11 environment states exclude protocols/monitors and imply no minimum cost. Eventual handover uses theoretical FLTL beyond native safety-$T$~\cite[Sec.~5.3]{Nahabedian2020}; this is a construction, not a native solver run.
