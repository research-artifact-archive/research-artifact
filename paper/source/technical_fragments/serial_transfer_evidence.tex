\paragraph{Finite checks of the transfer condition.}
A saved auxiliary check of the equal-alphabet specialization considers 4,352 two-component games and 16,640 all-old starting tuples: 256 exhaustive one-state cases and 4,096 seeded two-state cases. The latter are the first 4,096 of 10,695 generated candidates having a nonempty quiescent all-new target set; 6,599 outside that domain are retained as excluded counts. The check uses ordinary controls, UC events, nondeterministic outcomes, partial transfers and arbitrary nonempty target subsets. It finds 1,910 both-WIN starts, 63 fine-only WIN starts and 14,667 both-LOSS starts, with no coarse-only WIN. It also checks 6,541 partial-prefix quiescence obligations and 5,124 Cartesian outcome comparisons. Monitors and precedence are absent and endpoint controllers are not reconstructed, so this does not test the commuting-monitor, precedence or frontend premises. Seed 20261003 and both failed-condition counterexamples are retained with the checker. This is a finite sanity check of the stated implication, not its proof, an application benchmark or a performance result.
