# Exact minimax additive regret from the threshold endpoint

Use precisely the partial-repair workflow operation and observation interface of paper152, with nonnegative common call cost kappa, global extra-call allowance r, finite DAG and finite footprints. Let K(B) be the informed minimax curve and C(P,B) the worst cost of one universally capped, budget-unaware deterministic policy. No information about the actual repeated-write count is returned.

Claim: delta*=inf_P sup_{B>=0}(C(P,B)-K(B))=-Theta(J,r,0)>=0, where Theta has terminal target K(e), as in the paper. The minimum is attained and its policy is extractable using the shifted inequalities a<=Theta(S,t,e)+delta*.

For any terminal visible history h, its least consistent write count m(h) is the sum of each observed dirty set's minimum footprint count. Fresh preparation and independent own-job writes realize this least count; every larger bound permits the history using fewer than its allowed writes. Thus sup_B(C(P,B)-K(B)) equals sup_terminal_h(cost(h)-K(m(h))). For the forward inequality, any history feasible at B has m(h)<=B and K nondecreasing. For the reverse inequality, the finite minimally realizing environment at B=m(h) is itself allowed. Every policy has finite depth at most n+r and finite observation/action branching after the model reduction, so suprema are maxima and the strategy minimum is attained.

K saturates at Cmax by E=Sigma+r max sigma_i. Replace m by min(m,E): every future increment commutes with clipping, including c(D)>sigma_i, and targets are constant beyond E. No accumulated cost is clipped. For any real delta, a continuation with cost(h)<=K(m(h))+delta for all terminal histories has exactly the feasibility recurrence of the paper with terminal values K(e)+delta. Max, min and constant subtraction commute with a uniform additive shift. Induction on |S|+t therefore gives Theta_delta=Theta+delta at every state. Initial feasibility is equivalent to0<=Theta_root+delta; its smallest delta is -Theta_root. Informed minimax cannot exceed any fixed policy's worst cost at a given budget, so that minimum is nonnegative.

Use a maximizing job from the original table. After the observed dirty set, choose an accepting or rejecting child satisfying the shifted inequality. Such a child exists by the recurrence. This supplies a budget-unaware policy with worst additive regret delta*. A smaller claimed tolerance fails the root inequality and induces an adversarial finite observation continuation for every attempted choice. Independent per-completion constants mu_i add the same sum to every completed play and informed comparator; they cancel from regret, as long as they are not charged on rejection.

This is an additional interpretation and policy use of a finite minimax threshold calculation, not a new general game-solving principle or a closed form for all workflows. It quantifies the amount of loss when exact simultaneous optimality fails. The whole-kernel retained-record program class is a different interface; its general workflow minimax-loss problem is not solved by this argument.
